Privacy Policy

YouTube Workstream · Last updated October 10, 2026

YouTube Workstream ("the app") is a tool that runs on the creator's own computer to prepare videos and upload them privately to the creator's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what Google and YouTube data the app accesses and what it does with that data.

Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests these permissions for your YouTube account:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload your video files.
  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube): to identify your channel, list your existing playlists, and add uploaded videos to them.
  • Manage captions (youtube.force-ssl): to upload a caption track for a video you uploaded.

How the data is used

  • Your data is used only to perform the actions you ask for: uploading your video as Private, setting its title, description, and tags, adding it to playlists you already have, and uploading its captions.
  • The app does not publish, schedule, or delete videos, and does not create playlists.
  • The app does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone.

Where data is stored

  • The OAuth sign-in token is stored only on your computer, in the app's local secrets folder. It is never sent to the developer or any other server except Google's.
  • Transcripts, titles, descriptions, and other outputs are saved only on your computer, in a folder next to your video.
  • The developer runs no server and receives none of your data.

Third-party services

The app sends data directly to Google's YouTube Data API to perform uploads. If you choose to generate text with an AI provider (such as Anthropic or OpenAI) using your own API key, video transcript text is sent to that provider under its own terms. Google user data obtained through the YouTube API is not sent to AI providers.

By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through YouTube's security settings.
  • Deleting the app's secrets/token.json file removes the stored sign-in from your computer. Deleting a video's output folder removes its generated files.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

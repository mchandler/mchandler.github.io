YouTube Workstream

A personal post-production tool for a creator's own YouTube channel.

What it does

After a video is finished, YouTube Workstream runs on the creator's own computer to:

  • transcribe the video locally, with timestamps
  • suggest titles and thumbnail text
  • write a video description with chapter markers
  • upload the video to the creator's own channel as Private, add it to the creator's existing playlists, and attach captions

It never publishes or schedules videos. The creator reviews everything and publishes manually in YouTube Studio.

Google and YouTube access

The tool signs in to the creator's YouTube account through Google OAuth so it can upload videos, read the channel's playlists, add videos to them, and upload captions. Sign-in credentials are stored only on the creator's computer. See the Privacy Policy for details.
